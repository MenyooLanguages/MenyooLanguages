## How to get listed

1. Fix anything in the pack — wording, a term that clashes with your game's official localization, a truncated string.
2. Open a PR that also adds your name/alias (and optional link) to the row of your language in this file:

   ```md
   | 🇩🇪 | Deutsch | `German.json` | reviewed | [@your-alias](https://github.com/your-alias) |
   ```

3. Prefer not to use git? Open an issue with the fix; the row will be updated for you.

## Status meanings

| Status | Meaning |
|:--|:--|
| hand-written, menu-verified | translated by hand and checked against the in-game menu |
| machine-assisted, native review wanted | structurally complete, produced with a machine engine, no native pass yet |
| reviewed | at least one native speaker went through the pack |
| native | written and maintained by a native speaker |
